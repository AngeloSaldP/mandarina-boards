Route 2

The live 3D book, with the Blender look

The spike's three.js book, dressed in textures baked from the Blender scene: the desk plate without the book, the book's shadow, the cover (leather, deboss and seal), the inside of the cover, the page paper and the gilt edge. The camera is the hero camera. Until the WebGL scene is ready, the hero still stands in for it.

Loading the book

Add ?bench=1 to the address for the 30-second page-turn benchmark (the same one the owner's iPhone ran on the spike).

Decision: route 2, the live book

Bytes
  • JS 139.6 KB gzip: the three.js chunk is 135.6 KB of that.
  • Textures: 418.6 KB, nine WebP (q85) files: desk plates 21.9 and 26.4 KB, shadows 71.9 and 98.6 KB, covers 97.8 and 90.2 KB, inside 5.0 KB, paper 5.1 KB, gilt 1.8 KB.
  • Poster until ready: 20.8 KB.
  • GPU memory, uncompressed with mipmaps: about 59 MB.
Frame time
30 s page-turn benchmark: 4,322 frames, p50 6.9 ms, p95 7.0 ms, 2 frames over 16.7 ms (headed Chromium, Intel Iris Xe (D3D11), 1440x900, a 144 Hz display). The owner's iPhone ran the untextured spike at 58.5 fps; a mid-range Android is unmeasured.
What it needs
WebGL with a fine pointer (the spec §6.2 tier rules), the baked textures from art/scene/bake_live.py (96 s of GPU to rebake), and a fallback still. Hand-off from the still: mean pixel difference 1.1 of 255 at desktop, 2.9 on a Pixel 7.
Spec lines it changes
  • §5.1 "no WebGL on the home": breaks if this runs on the home. It fits the catalog (170 KB JS budget, §10.1).
  • §10.2 textures: these are WebP; the spec wants KTX2/Basis (KTX2 would also cut the GPU memory from about 59 MB, against the 64 MB cap).
  • §10.2 named imports: adds BoxGeometry, Group, Mesh, MeshBasicMaterial, MeshStandardMaterial and TextureLoader.
  • "No glTF" holds: the geometry is built in code and the props are a texture.
  • §6.3: the 1000 ms camera move on opening is left out to keep the desk plate aligned; the open cover lies over the stamp and envelope, which the open Blender variant moves aside.
  • §7.4: the live seal is a fade (no segments or embers).
