Route 3

Hybrid: frames first, then the live book

Scrolling scrubs the pre-rendered approach. Its last frame is the hero camera, so at the end of the scroll the live book, loaded quietly after load, takes over on the same camera without a jump. "Romper el sello" plays the pre-rendered seal break over it, then the live cover opens and the pages turn.

Scroll to scrub

Decision: route 3, hybrid

Bytes
  • Approach frames: 1,275 KB desktop (1,564 KB phone).
  • Seal break: 843 KB.
  • Textures: 419 KB.
  • JS: 141 KB gzip, three.js included.
  • Everything after frame 1 (6 KB) loads after load.
Frame time
Scrubbing with the live book loaded underneath: p50 6.9 ms, p95 7.0 ms, no frame over 16.7 ms (headed Chromium, Intel Iris Xe (D3D11), 1440x900, a 144 Hz display). Turning pages afterwards is the live route's figure.
What it needs
Both pipelines: Blender frames for the approach and the seal, and baked textures for the live book. They must be rendered from the same scene and grade. Measured hand-offs: approach frame 96 to live, mean difference 1.1 of 255 (desktop) and 2.9 (Pixel 7); seal frame 37 to live, 2.2 and 3.4. No visible jump.
Spec lines it changes
  • All of route 1's changes, plus route 2's.
  • On the home it breaks §5.1 (no WebGL) and both of the home's budgets in §10.1: 60 KB of JS and 250 KB of images.
  • As the catalog's entrance on desktop, it fits the 170 KB JS budget, but not the 1.8 MB texture budget once the frames count as images.
